# Supervisor approval request: relevance annotation protocol

**Status:** Prepared for review; not sent or approved.  
**Protocol under review:** [`annotation_protocol_draft.md`](annotation_protocol_draft.md)

Please review and approve or request changes to these points before any independent relevance ratings begin:

1. The 0–3 ordinal rubric measures seasonal suitability for the named destination, activity, and month.
2. Seasonal suitability is rated separately from legal access, restrictions, and closure status.
3. Each grade requires a direct, destination- and month-relevant source, retrieval/review date, and concise rationale. If support is unavailable, annotators mark evidence missing and do not guess a grade.
4. Reviewers receive a blind packet without existing grades, rationales, method rankings, or scores, and complete ratings independently.
5. Disagreements remain visible; adjudication follows a predeclared rule and is not performed by averaging ordinal scores by default.
