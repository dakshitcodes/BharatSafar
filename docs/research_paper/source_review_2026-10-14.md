## Policy consequence

All 11 current records remain `unverified`; none has a `lastVerifiedAt` date or complete root-level effective-date interval accepted by the runtime policy. The partial records also lack explicit destination activity applicability. The policy therefore rejects them for ranking effects and the benchmark reports zero evidence-backed disruptions. This does not establish that no restrictions exist; it means this audit did not locate sufficient current, claim-matching evidence for the supplied records.

No source was copied into the dataset as proof of a claim it does not support. All source checks are dated and record-specific in `research_data/disruption_evidence_register.csv`.
